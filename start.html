How is your RTL kept safe?

Your design is the company. These rules are written into our NDA, and we follow them whether or not your NDA asks for them.

  1. NDA before code. We see nothing confidential until a mutual NDA is signed by both sides.
  2. Your systems first. We work on your VPN or machines when you offer them. Otherwise files stay on encrypted, password-protected devices only.
  3. No public AI tools on your code. Your RTL, specs and waveforms never go into ChatGPT, Copilot or any public AI service unless you say yes in writing.
  4. Nothing reused. Client code never appears in other projects, including our open-source repository.
  5. Deleted when you ask. On request, or when the work ends, every copy is returned or securely deleted within 15 days, confirmed in writing.
