Questions people ask

The honest answers.

You're a new company. Why should I trust you?

Fair question, and we won't pretend otherwise. That's why the first week is free and our code is public. You see the quality of the work before you spend a rupee or a dollar.

Will my RTL stay private?

Yes. We sign a mutual NDA before we see anything (yours or ours), can work on your machines or VPN, and never reuse client code in our open-source work.

Do you need our commercial simulator licences?

No. We can work fully on open-source tools like Icarus Verilog, Verilator, cocotb and SymbiYosys. When you have VCS, Xcelium or Questa, we write UVM that runs on them.

How is pricing decided?

After the health check we quote a fixed price per block, or a monthly retainer for ongoing work. You know the cost before we start, and payment is split half up front and half on delivery.

Is Neesil a registered business?

Yes. Neesil is registered as an MSME (Udyam) in Telangana, India, and invoices properly for work in India and abroad.

Do you work with teams outside India?

Yes. We work remotely and keep a few hours of daily overlap with your time zone.
