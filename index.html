Why "Neesil"

Your silicon. Checked like it's ours.

Neesil is my name, Maneesh, folded into silicon. It also happens to say "your silicon" in Telugu, and that is exactly how we work. Your chip is your team's months of effort, your funding and your deadline. We treat every block you send us as if our own name were printed on the die.

I started Neesil because small chip teams in India and around the world are building great designs with very few people. They rarely have a full verification team or a budget for every EDA licence. That's where bugs slip through. We want to be the verification team they can afford.

Questions people ask

The honest answers.

You're a new company. Why should I trust you?

Fair question. That's why the first week is free and our code is public. You see our work before you spend a rupee or a dollar.

Will my RTL stay private?

Yes. We sign your NDA before we see anything, work on your machines or VPN if you prefer, and never reuse client code in our open-source work.

Do you need our commercial simulator licences?

No. We can work fully on open-source tools, and we write UVM that runs on VCS, Xcelium or Questa when you have them.

Do you work with teams outside India?

Yes. We work remotely across time zones and keep a few hours of overlap with your team every day.
