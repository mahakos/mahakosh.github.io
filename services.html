What can you hand over?

One block, one problem, or the whole regression. Each service below ends in files you keep and can run without us.

  • Verification health check

    1 week, one block

    We read your spec, RTL and current tests, then plant hundreds of realistic bugs in a copy of the RTL, one at a time, to see which ones your regression actually catches. Bugs that cannot change anything visible are proved harmless with a formal tool and not counted against you. You get a short written report: what is covered, what is missing, and where we would look for bugs first.

    Read a sample report.

    You get

    • Coverage and test review
    • Mutation results: which planted bugs got through
    • For each bug that got through, the input sequence that exposes it and a ready-to-run test that catches it, checked in simulation
    • Your tests ranked: which catch the most bugs, and which add nothing
    • Ranked findings with fixes
    • A fixed quote for the next step, if you want one

  • Testbench build

    UVM, cocotb, SystemVerilog

    A complete environment around your block, from scratch or on top of what you have. Agents, drivers, monitors, scoreboards and a reference model written from the spec, not from your RTL, so it can disagree with the design when the design is wrong.

    You get

    • Test plan you review before coding starts
    • Testbench source in your repository
    • Constrained-random and directed tests
    • One-command regression

  • Coverage closure and bug hunting

    Functional and code coverage

    Your tests pass and the dashboard says 95%. We find out what the missing 5% hides and whether the covered 95% is measuring the right things, then write the stimulus and checks to close it honestly.

    You get

    • Coverage model tied to the spec, with crosses
    • Every hole closed or explained in writing
    • Bug reports with failing seed, waveform and suggested fix

  • Assertions and formal verification

    SVA, SymbiYosys, k-induction

    Handshake and control bugs often need a sequence that random simulation never hits. Properties catch them early. On our own AXI4-Lite block, a formal proof caught two bugs that every simulation test missed, in under two seconds.

    See that case study.

    You get

    • Protocol assertions (APB, AXI, SPI, custom)
    • Formal setup for control logic, FIFOs and arbiters
    • Bounded checks and full proofs where they converge

  • Regression and CI setup

    Make, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins

    Tests only protect you if they run on every change. We set up lint, multi-seed regression, formal runs and coverage merging in your CI, so a broken commit is flagged in minutes, not at the next milestone.

    You get

    • Lint, simulation and formal on every push
    • Seed tracking so any failure can be rerun
    • A short README your team can follow

  • Overflow verification for design-services firms

    White-label, under your NDA

    When a project spike lands and your verification engineers are booked, we take a block under your process, your tools and your name. You stay the client's single point of contact.

    You get

    • Work in your repository and coding style
    • Daily or weekly status in your format
    • No contact with your client unless you ask
